Identity · three directions

Kinscope marks

All three use the app's teal and ink and set the wordmark in Bricolage Grotesque. Aperture has been redrawn: no closed ring, no centre dot, nothing that reads as a target.

01 · Aperture, revised
Focus, not crosshair
Kinscope
18px, two arcs

Three open arcs, each thinner in sweep than a full ring and rotated a third of a turn from the last. Nothing closes, nothing centres, so the eye reads depth pulling inward rather than a sight lining something up. The innermost arc is teal: the clear view is the thing you arrive at.

02 · Overlap
Where two people meet
Kinscope
18px

Two circles for two people, and the teal is the part they hold in common. Closest to the shared-household idea the app is built on.

03 · Monogram
K in a soft disc
Kinscope
18px

A K held inside a filled disc, round caps to keep it warm rather than technical. The most app-icon-native of the three, and the only one that carries the name without the wordmark.
